Project Kanban Board

Plan, assign and track your team’s work

What this app does

Project Kanban Board helps your team plan and track work in one place. Tasks move across four columns: To Do, In Progress, Review and Done. Each task has a type, an assignee, a due date and context notes, and cards change color as due dates get closer so you can see what needs attention at a glance.

How to use it

  1. Open Kanban in the navbar to see the board.
  2. Select New task to add a task with a title, description, type (feature or bug), status, assignee, due date and context.
  3. Drag a card to another column, or click it and change its status, to show progress.
  4. Click a card to edit it or delete it. Use Copy context on a card or in the task window to copy the title, description and context to your clipboard.
  5. Use the four cards above the board to keep links to your presentation, report, demo and documentation.
  6. Open Members to add or remove team members. Removing someone unassigns their tasks.
  7. Open Settings to change the theme, font and text size.

What the card colors mean

  • Due in more than two days
  • Due within two days
  • Overdue
  • Done

Your data

Tasks, members, links and settings are saved in this browser, so they stay when you reload the page. They aren’t shared with other people or devices.
